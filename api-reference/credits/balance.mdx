## What the balance includes

`balance` is the company's total available balance: regular credits plus unexpired one-time credits. `currency` is `credits`, not a payment currency. The response does not include package breakdowns or expiry dates; view those details in the app.

See [Credits & billing](/learn/credits) for spending order, renewal, and purchases.
